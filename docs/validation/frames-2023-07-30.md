# CHARIS 2023-07-30 (UT) — definitive frame lists

Sources: Excel observing log (`Europa Subaru_CHARIS Observations 7_29 — Target List and Log.xlsx`, sheet `Log`, 19 non-blank rows) and a header survey of every `CRSA*.fits` / `*_cube.fits` under `/home/shared/exoserver/Subaru-Europa/2023-07-30/` on mueller (620 files: 535 top-level raw frames 123399–123933, 8 raw copies in `BD343631-broadband-unpol/`, 77 cubes). Per-frame table: [frames-2023-07-30-headers.csv](frames-2023-07-30-headers.csv) (one row per file; column `log_role_from_analysis` is my classification). Survey script and the original CSV: `~/charis-val/datasets/survey_headers.py`, `header_survey_2023-07-30.csv` on mueller.

Frame IDs below are the last 6 digits of `CRSA00123xxx`; log ranges like `123488-51` mean 123488–123551.

**Header facts that differ from the task brief**
- Raw frames are **not 2-D**: primary HDU has NAXIS=0 and holds all the header keywords; the data are 4–136 compressed read extensions (11 reads for a 16 s frame). Cubes are 22×201×201 with 1–2 extensions. All keywords below were read from the primary header.
- Raw headers use `FILTER01`, `Y_PRISM`, `Y_SHUTTR`, `UT`; there is **no `FILTNAME`, `UTC-TIME` or `PARANG` in raw frames** (those exist in cubes only). Cubes have **no `RET-ANG1`**, so HWP angles must come from the raw frames.
- `UT-END`/`MJD-END` in the raw primary header span only the first read (1.48 s), not the exposure; use `EXPTIME`.

**Which HD 236894 range is clean.** The log's "all had HWP fixed" (123488–551) and "fixed HWP, sequences start here" (123552–583) are only partly borne out. Header history (`RET-ANG1`):
- 123488–490: fixed at 0 (123488 is the 7.4 s frame).
- 123491–519: cycling but with repeated/missed steps (67.5 repeated at 123494 and 123498); the segment 123502–517 (16 frames, 4 cycles) happens to be clean in the header.
- 123520–548: **fixed at 22.5 for 29 frames**.
- 123549–555: resumes cycling; 123552–555 is one full cycle; **123556 repeats 67.5 (missed step)**; 123557–559 partial cycle.
- **123560–583: clean, 0-start, 6 complete cycles.** This is the range I recommend. The 32 extracted cubes cover 123552–583, so cubes for 123552–559 contain the glitchy frames.

The HWP angle label also changes within a ramp: the extension headers of frame N list the *next* angle in the last read(s) (e.g. 123560: `0.0;45.0`), so the waveplate appears to move before the ramp ends; it is unresolved how many reads are affected (§5).

**Exposure times.** Header EXPTIME matches the log wherever the log gives one (60.5, 5.9, 16.2, 30.98, 25.08, 13.28), and the log's short first frames are confirmed: HD 236419 first two frames 16.2/7.4 s, HIP 3373 123476 5.9 s, HD 236894 123488 7.4 s, H-band 123584 20.65 s. Log "first frame 7 s / 6 s" are 7.38 / 5.9 s; "sequence starts at 85" for the H-band set is **not** consistent with headers (HWP still stuck at 0/45 until 123597).

**Satellite spots.** `X_GRDST` = `OFF` in **all 620 files** (`X_GRDAMP`=0.05 µm, `X_GRDSEP`=15.5 λ/D, `X_GRDMOD`=1000 Hz are just stored defaults). No frame in the night, standard or Europa, has astrogrid spots, so **none of the datasets can use spot-based registration**. No other keyword containing GRD/GRID/ASTRO/MODAMP exists.

**Sky frames.** The log's four sky-flat rows match header `DATA-TYP=SKYFLAT` exactly (frame range and filter/exposure: BB 13.28 s, H 30.98 s). Headers do not tell the 6″-NW sky (14:51–14:58 UT) from twilight (15:37–15:45 UT) apart except by UT. All 22 sky frames carry `OBJECT=HD239068`; D_IMR tracking state is TRACK/NSID-ADI.

**Object name.** `OBJECT` = `HD239068` for **all** frames 123609–123933, including the broadband Europa science run and all sky frames; the log flags this only for the H-band Europa frames. Do not select by `OBJECT` for Europa or sky.

**Download state.** Every frame 123399–123933 (535) is present on mueller, contiguous, last written 2026-11-04 16:12 PST. Cubes exist for BD+34 3631 123422 (1), HD 236419 123432–475 (44) and HD 236894 123552–583 (32) only. Not extracted to cubes: HIP 3373, HD 236894 123488–551 and H-band 584–608, HD 239068, Europa, sky frames. The 8 raw copies and the cube under `BD343631-broadband-unpol/` are truncated (1 read, ~5.5 MB vs ~57 MB for the top-level copy), which is what astropy warned about; they are flagged in the CSV (`truncated_copy`). **Frames missing on mueller relative to the log: none** (the log ends at 123933). I could not read `zadmin/filelist.txt` / `inbound.txt` to confirm nothing beyond 123933 is expected, because mueller became unreachable.

## 5. Open questions
1. **Which flat does the IDL pipeline expect?** Only 5 lamp PDI flat frames at 5.9 s (bright) and no lamp-off frames; twilight sky flats (BB 123926–933; H 123920–925, partial cycle) are the other candidate. Not settled from the data.
2. **Dark matching:** the only darks are 200.6 s; none match 5.9/13.3/16.2 s. Existing cubes used `BGSUB=F`.
3. **HWP truth:** is `RET-ANG1` the real waveplate state? It contradicts the log's "HWP fixed" for HIP 3373 and HD 236894 123488–551, and the next angle shows up in the last read(s) of each ramp. How many reads see the move, and whether cubes built from reads 1–11 are affected, needs a per-read check on mueller.
4. `RET-POS1` (stage angle, SYNCHRO_ADI mode) drifts with time and does not map simply onto `RET-ANG1`; I did not interpret it.
5. Whether the 6″-NW sky (123848–859) is clean of Europa/Jupiter light: header pointing offsets were not tabulated.
6. HD 236894 H-band "sequence starts at 85": header says the HWP was stuck until 123597; the log note looks off by 12 frames.
7. Wavecal coverage: H-band has a single 1550 nm frame pair; unknown whether the IDL H-band calibration needs more.
8. Anything past 123933, and a full truncation check of every file, could not be run (mueller unreachable at the end).
9. Header `SEEING` is not usable (6.79″ on frames where the log's DIMM is 0.6″); the raw `AIRMASS` is available per frame in the CSV.
